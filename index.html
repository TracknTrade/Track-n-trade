2. The maths

We have checked every calculator in the app against worked examples. But every result depends entirely on the numbers you enter and the assumptions you set — target weight, daily gain, costs, interest rate, sale price and the rest. A wrong, stale or missing input gives a wrong answer. You must check any figure yourself before you rely on it.

6. What we collect

Free version: to keep it free, the free version sends us an anonymised summary of each lot you record — date and hour, saleyard, head, average weight, price and whether it's $/hd or $/kg, stock type, markings, store or prime — with a random device number so we can count distinct users. People & places
Buyer Way

Who each lot is bought for — a client, or Self if you're buying direct. They show as one-tap chips on the Sale tab, so keep the names short: JS, Smith or Back Ck fits far more on the row than "J & M Smith Pastoral Co" and you'll still know who's who. Tap a buyer way to open its profile: the drop-off map pin goes into the driver's text, the preferred carrier is picked for you on the Truck tab, and email and phone are kept for reference.

Markings

Paint-mark chips on the Purchase recorder. Defaults are NM (no mark), R, S, L and T — rump, shoulder, loin, tail — short so they fit on one row. NM always stands alone; the others can combine. Add more here, or on the fly from the recorder.
