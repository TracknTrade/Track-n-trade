Agistment cost calculator

Optional — work this out if you don't already know your $/wk agistment-equivalent cost.

Takes your whole farm's yearly running cost, spreads it across the head you turn off (sell or run) each year, then divides by 52 weeks. The result is a $/head/week figure directly comparable to Agistment $/wk above — if you run your own land instead of paying an outside agistment provider, it's automatically filled into that field for you as you type below.
