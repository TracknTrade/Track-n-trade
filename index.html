People & places
Buyer Way

Who each lot is bought for — a client, or Self if you're buying direct. They show as one-tap chips on the Sale tab, so keep the names short: JS, Smith or Back Ck fits far more on the row than "J & M Smith Pastoral Co" and you'll still know who's who. Tap a buyer way to open its profile: the drop-off map pin goes into the driver's text, the preferred carrier is picked for you on the Truck tab, and email and phone are kept for reference.

Markings

Paint-mark options shown on the Purchase recorder, like Shoulder, Loin, Rump, Tail — or No Mark by default. Add more here, or on the fly from the recorder.

Account
Team sync

Your name is stamped on each lot you record, so an office running several buyers can tell whose lots are whose in the export and the shared spreadsheet. The licence code is for paid team/district licences — leave it blank and nothing changes.

No licence code entered.

Backup

Everything in this app lives on this phone. Back up makes one file — settings, buyer ways, markings, carriers, sign-off, the current sale's lots, watchlist and past sales — and hands it to your share sheet (AirDrop, Files, Drive, Mail). Restore loads that file onto a new phone and replaces what's there. Pen-card photos aren't included.
